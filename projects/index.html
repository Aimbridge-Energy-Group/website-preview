Projects

Selected installations built around Aimbridge microgrids.

The STEAD School

At The STEAD School's Animal Sciences Center, we built a small off-grid microgrid that combines solar generation, battery storage and a backup generator. It doubles as a hands-on learning system: students collect energy data from the microgrid and study how power is produced, stored and used.

Auto Aves

At Auto Aves' 28,000-square-foot dealership in Lakewood, Colorado, we installed a 36 kW solar array and low-voltage DC lighting throughout the building, powered by an Aimbridge DC-coupled microgrid.

Pure Harvest Cannabis Dispensary

Pure Harvest operates this retail establishment year-round on power from an Aimbridge microgrid. We installed LED lighting throughout, creating a bright retail environment.
