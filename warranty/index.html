1. Warranty Overview

DC Energy Group, LLC., dba Aimbridge Energy Group ("Aimbridge"), warrants that its manufactured electrical equipment will be free from defects in materials, workmanship, and manufacturing-related integration under normal use and operation for the applicable warranty period described below.

This Limited Product Warranty ("Warranty") applies to new Aimbridge-manufactured products sold directly by Aimbridge or through authorized distribution and sales channels.

This Warranty is subject to the conditions, limitations, and exclusions described herein and should be read in conjunction with Aimbridge's Standard Terms and Conditions of Sale.

2. Products Covered

This Warranty applies to Aimbridge-manufactured equipment only.

For integrated products incorporating third-party equipment, this Warranty covers Aimbridge's own design, integration, assembly, and manufacturing workmanship, subject to the third-party component provisions below.

Products manufactured by third parties and resold or supplied by Aimbridge, including SmartFlower™ equipment, are covered only to the extent expressly stated in the applicable product warranty or sales agreement.

3. Warranty Period

The standard Warranty period is:

Twelve (12) months from the date of initial commissioning, not to exceed eighteen (18) months from the date of shipment by Aimbridge, whichever results in the earlier expiration date.

For purposes of this Warranty:

  • "Commissioning" means the initial successful installation, energization, and functional verification of the equipment for its intended application.
  • Commissioning must be documented by the installer or customer and may be subject to verification by Aimbridge.
  • If a commissioning date cannot be reasonably established, the warranty period will be deemed to begin on the shipment date and expire twelve (12) months thereafter.
  • Warranty repairs or replacement components do not restart or extend the original Warranty period. Replacement components remain covered for the balance of the original Warranty period or ninety (90) days after replacement, whichever is longer.

Extended warranties, if offered, must be expressly documented in a separate written agreement.

4. Warranty Coverage

During the applicable Warranty period, Aimbridge warrants against defects attributable to its manufacturing, assembly, or integration activities, including:

  • Defective cabinet assembly or internal wiring.
  • Defective electrical connections or manufacturing-related component installation.
  • Defects in Aimbridge-manufactured electrical distribution or control assemblies.
  • Defects in Aimbridge-developed control functionality that cause the equipment to materially fail to operate according to its published specifications.
  • Other manufacturing-related defects affecting the normal operation of the equipment.

Coverage is limited to defects that arise under normal operating conditions and within the equipment's documented electrical, mechanical, and environmental specifications.

5. Warranty Remedies

If Aimbridge determines that a product has a defect covered under this Warranty, Aimbridge will, at its discretion:

  1. Repair the defective equipment or component.
  2. Provide a replacement component or equivalent replacement equipment.
  3. Authorize another reasonable corrective action, including remote technical assistance or field-directed repair.

If repair or replacement is not commercially practicable, Aimbridge may offer a refund or credit for the affected equipment, subject to applicable law.

Replacement components may be new or functionally equivalent refurbished components that meet applicable specifications.

Unless otherwise required by law, Aimbridge's warranty obligations are limited to the repair, replacement, or other remedy expressly authorized by Aimbridge under this Warranty.

6. Third-Party Components and Manufacturer Warranties

Aimbridge equipment may incorporate components manufactured by third parties, including inverters, battery systems, charge controllers, power conversion devices, communication equipment, and other electrical components.

Where a third-party component fails due to a defect in that component, the applicable original equipment manufacturer's ("OEM") warranty will govern any manufacturer-specific or extended coverage.

During the Aimbridge Warranty period, Aimbridge will provide reasonable assistance in identifying and coordinating claims involving components installed as part of an Aimbridge-manufactured system.

Aimbridge's own Warranty continues to apply where a failure is attributable to defective Aimbridge integration, assembly, or workmanship, even when a third-party component is involved.

Any warranty coverage extending beyond the Aimbridge Warranty period is subject to the applicable OEM warranty and its terms, including eligibility, remedies, exclusions, and claim requirements.

Aimbridge does not independently extend or guarantee an OEM warranty unless expressly agreed in writing.

7. Battery and Energy Storage Equipment

Battery systems and energy storage components are subject to additional operating conditions and manufacturer-specific warranty requirements.

Battery cells, modules, battery management systems (BMS), and related energy storage components may be covered by separate OEM warranties.

Battery capacity retention, cycle life, degradation, and expected service life are governed by the applicable battery manufacturer's published warranty, where provided.

Aimbridge does not independently warrant battery capacity retention, energy throughput, or cycle life unless expressly specified in writing.

Battery installations must use compatible equipment and approved configurations and remain within applicable voltage, current, temperature, charging, and operating limits.

Failures attributable to incompatible batteries, unsupported firmware or BMS configurations, improper charging, excessive discharge, or operating conditions outside approved limits are excluded to the extent such conditions cause or contribute to the failure.

8. Software, Firmware, and Connectivity

Certain Aimbridge products incorporate embedded software, firmware, and network-connected control capabilities.

This Warranty covers reproducible defects in Aimbridge-developed embedded control functionality that materially prevent covered equipment from operating according to its published specifications.

Aimbridge may satisfy a covered software-related warranty claim through a software update, configuration correction, or another reasonable corrective action.

Software, firmware, and embedded code developed by third parties, including firmware embedded in or supplied with third-party inverters, battery systems, battery management systems (BMS), charge controllers, power conversion devices, communication equipment, and other OEM components, are not warranted by Aimbridge under this Warranty. Such software and firmware are governed solely by the applicable OEM's warranty, license terms, and support policies, and Aimbridge makes no representation regarding their performance, compatibility with future updates, or continued availability.

This Warranty does not cover failures caused by software or firmware updates, patches, or configuration changes that were not provided or approved by Aimbridge, or by third-party applications or software installed on or connected to the equipment.

Where a failure is attributable to defective Aimbridge-developed control functionality, Aimbridge's coverage under this Section 8 continues to apply even if a third-party component is involved.

This Warranty does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free software operation.

Cloud-based monitoring, analytics, remote connectivity, subscription services, and third-party communications networks are not covered under this equipment Warranty and are subject to applicable service agreements.

9. Warranty Exclusions

This Warranty does not cover defects, damage, or failures caused by:

  • Improper installation, wiring, commissioning, maintenance, or servicing.
  • Operation outside published electrical or environmental specifications.
  • Use with incompatible or unapproved batteries, inverters, loads, or other connected equipment.
  • Unauthorized alterations or modifications that cause or contribute to a product failure.
  • Installation, modification, or updating of software or firmware (including patches, configuration changes, or third-party applications) that was not provided or approved by Aimbridge.
  • Misuse, negligence, abuse, or accidental damage.
  • Water intrusion, flooding, fire, lightning, power surges, or other external events, except where a covered manufacturing defect caused the condition.
  • Improper transportation, handling, or storage after delivery.
  • Normal wear, cosmetic deterioration, or consumable components.
  • Failure to follow applicable installation, operation, or maintenance instructions.
  • External utility disturbances, grid conditions, generator problems, or third-party electrical system failures.
  • Damage resulting from inadequate ventilation, excessive ambient temperatures, corrosive environments, or other conditions outside product specifications.

This Warranty also excludes performance variations attributable to solar availability, weather conditions, site-specific conditions, load profiles, battery degradation, or other factors outside Aimbridge's control.

An exclusion applies only to the extent the excluded condition causes or contributes to the claimed defect or failure.

10. Installation Labor and Field Service

Unless expressly included in a separate written agreement, this Warranty covers the applicable equipment remedy but does not include:

  • Equipment removal or reinstallation labor.
  • Electrician, contractor, or technician labor charges.
  • Travel expenses or on-site service visits.
  • Crane, lift, rigging, or other specialized equipment.
  • Building modifications or restoration.
  • Equipment rental, substitute power, or temporary power systems.

Aimbridge may elect to provide remote technical assistance to support diagnosis, repair, or replacement.

Any field service, installation, or commissioning warranty provided under a separate Aimbridge project agreement will be governed by that agreement.

11. Warranty Claims and RMA Process

Customers requesting warranty support must contact Aimbridge and provide sufficient information to identify the equipment and reported issue.

Warranty requests should include:

  • Customer name and contact information.
  • Product model and serial number.
  • Original purchase or order reference.
  • Shipment and commissioning dates, if available.
  • Description of the reported issue, including error codes or operating conditions.
  • Relevant photographs, diagnostic logs, or other reasonably available supporting information.

Aimbridge may perform remote diagnostics or request additional information before determining an appropriate remedy.

If a component return is required, Aimbridge will issue a Return Merchandise Authorization ("RMA") and provide instructions.

Products or components must not be returned without an authorized RMA.

Customers must follow reasonable safety, shutdown, disconnection, packaging, and handling instructions.

Shipping Responsibilities:

For an approved warranty claim, Aimbridge will pay standard return shipping for repaired or replacement components to the original equipment delivery location within the contiguous United States.

Customer is responsible for shipping defective components to Aimbridge or its designated service provider unless otherwise authorized in writing.

Special freight, expedited shipping, and on-site removal or installation costs are excluded unless expressly authorized by Aimbridge.

For equipment that cannot reasonably be returned, Aimbridge will determine an appropriate repair or replacement process.

12. Customer Responsibilities

To maintain eligibility for Warranty coverage, Customer must:

  • Install and operate equipment in accordance with applicable instructions and electrical codes.
  • Use appropriately qualified installation and service personnel.
  • Maintain required ventilation, environmental conditions, and equipment protections.
  • Use supported equipment configurations and approved operating parameters.
  • Maintain reasonable records of commissioning, service, and maintenance.
  • Promptly notify Aimbridge after discovering a suspected defect and take reasonable steps to prevent further damage.
  • Provide reasonable access to diagnostic information needed to evaluate a claim.

Failure to satisfy a requirement does not automatically void the entire Warranty, but coverage may be denied to the extent the failure causes or materially contributes to the claimed defect or prevents reasonable verification of coverage.

13. Warranty Transferability

The Warranty remains associated with the original product serial number and installation location.

Transfer to a subsequent owner requires written notification to Aimbridge and its approval, which will not be unreasonably withheld where the equipment remains at its original installation site and continues to operate in an approved configuration.

Any transfer does not extend or restart the original Warranty period.
