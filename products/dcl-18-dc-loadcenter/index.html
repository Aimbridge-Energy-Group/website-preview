DC Power

DCL-18 DC Loadcenter

The DCL-18 combines DC/DC power conversion, distribution and branch circuit protection in one recessed wall-mount panel, with up to 18 plug-in breakers on a split A/B bus that can convert to a single bus. Each bus has its own 3 kW buck-boost converter, delivering regulated 12, 24 or 48 VDC from a 9–60 VDC battery.

Battery input
9–60 VDC
Regulated output
12, 24 or 48 VDC
Output power
3000 W per bus
Branch positions
18
