1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how DC Energy Group, LLC, dba Aimbridge Energy Group ("Aimbridge," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you visit our website, submit an inquiry, request information, register for a webinar or event, communicate with us, or otherwise interact with our online services that link to this Policy (collectively, the "Services").

This Policy is intended for website visitors, prospective customers, business contacts, partners, vendors, and other users of the Services. Separate notices or contractual terms may apply to employees, job applicants, customers using a specific product or service, or information processed on behalf of a customer.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Services, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact and business information, such as your name, business email address, telephone number, company, title, mailing address, and information you choose to include in a message or form.
  • Request and relationship information, such as product interests, project details, sales inquiries, meeting or event registrations, correspondence, and records of our business interactions.
  • Device and usage information, such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring and exit pages, pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, timestamps, and similar diagnostic or analytics data.
  • Cookie and similar technology data, including identifiers used to operate the website, remember preferences, measure site performance, and, if enabled, support analytics or marketing.
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide to us.

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect information directly from you, automatically from your browser or device, and from service providers or business partners that support our operations. For example, information may be collected when you complete a contact form, request a proposal or product information, communicate by email, register for a business event, or browse pages on the website.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information for legitimate business purposes, including to:

  • Respond to inquiries, provide requested information, and communicate about products, services, projects, or business opportunities.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Services and our business operations.
  • Analyze website traffic, usage, and performance and understand which content is useful to visitors.
  • Manage customer, prospect, partner, and vendor relationships and maintain business records.
  • Send business or marketing communications where permitted by law and honor applicable opt-out requests.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, security incidents, misuse, or unlawful activity.
  • Comply with applicable law, enforce agreements, establish or defend legal claims, and protect the rights, safety, and property of Aimbridge and others.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies. Some are necessary for website operation; others may support preferences, analytics, or marketing. Browser settings may allow you to block or delete cookies, but doing so can affect website functionality. If we deploy a cookie-consent or preference manager, your selections in that tool will govern supported optional technologies to the extent described there.

6. When We Disclose Information

We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

  • To service providers that perform functions for us, such as website hosting, cloud services, analytics, cybersecurity, customer relationship management, email delivery, event management, and professional services.
  • To affiliates, distributors, channel partners, contractors, or other business partners when appropriate to respond to your request or conduct a business relationship.
  • When required by law, legal process, regulation, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or property.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction, subject to applicable law.
  • With your direction or consent.

We do not describe information as “sold” or “shared” for targeted advertising unless our actual practices meet the relevant legal definition. If the website later uses advertising technologies or data practices that qualify as a sale or sharing under an applicable privacy law, this Policy and the website’s choice mechanisms will be updated before that use begins.

7. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to maintain business and transaction records, meet legal or contractual obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary based on the type of information, the relationship involved, and applicable legal requirements.

8. Washington Privacy and Data-Breach Considerations

Aimbridge is based in Washington State. Washington law defines specified categories of “personal information” for security-breach purposes and requires qualifying businesses to notify affected Washington residents when statutory conditions are met. Our incident-response procedures are intended to address applicable notification obligations.

Washington’s My Health My Data Act imposes separate requirements on businesses that collect or process “consumer health data,” including a specific consumer health data privacy policy and consent requirements in covered circumstances. Our ordinary commercial energy website is not intended to collect consumer health data. Please do not submit medical, health, biometric, or similar sensitive health information through general website forms. If our practices change so that the Act applies, we will implement the required disclosures and controls.
